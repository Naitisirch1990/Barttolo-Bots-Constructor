# Constructor 0.3.17

- Flowing water or lava in a railing position no longer rejects a bridge.
- Suspended sections crossing liquid above deck level receive a short covered gallery, with side walls, roof and raised roof-end lintels to divert runoff away from the entrances.
- The covered passage is temporarily plugged before excavation to remove flowing liquid and prevent water sources regenerating inside it.
- Dry sections retain open railings and existing bridge supports.
- Tests reproduce a 40-block ravine crossing at Y=45 through a waterfall and a lavafall. They let external fluid flow after completion and check the full walkway remains dry.
